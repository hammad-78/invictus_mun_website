Five diverse committees designed to challenge and inspire delegates at every level.

Beginner Friendly 60 Delegates

Organization of Islamic Cooperation

Intermediate 40 Delegates

The OIC committee brings together representatives from Muslim-majority nations to address issues of mutual concern, from humanitarian crises to economic cooperation and cultural preservation.

United Nations Security Council

Advanced 20 Delegates

The UNSC is the most prestigious committee at IV MUN, dealing with threats to international peace and security. With veto powers and crisis elements, this committee demands experienced, sharp delegates.

UN Human Rights Council

Intermediate 45 Delegates

The UNHRC addresses human rights violations worldwide, crafting resolutions that promote equality, justice, and dignity for all people. A committee for those passionate about human rights advocacy.
