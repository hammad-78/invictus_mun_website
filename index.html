About IV MUN

Redefining Diplomacy

Invictus Model United Nations is a premier MUN conference that brings together passionate delegates, seasoned chairs, and distinguished speakers for an unforgettable experience in international diplomacy and debate.

Our mission is to cultivate leadership, critical thinking, and global awareness among the youth, empowering them to become the changemakers of tomorrow.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Model United Nations?
Model United Nations is an educational simulation where students role-play as delegates to the UN and simulate UN committees. Participants develop critical thinking, public speaking, and diplomatic skills while discussing global issues.
Who can participate in IV MUN 2026?
IV MUN 2026 is open to university and high school students (with prior MUN experience for advanced committees). Whether you're a first-time delegate or a seasoned MUNer, we have a committee for you.
What is the dress code?
Formal Western or national dress is required. For gentlemen, a suit and tie. For ladies, formal business attire. National dress representing your assigned country is also encouraged and welcomed.
Is there a registration fee?
Yes, there is a nominal registration fee that covers conference materials, meals, social events, and certificates. Details regarding the fee structure and payment methods will be shared upon registration.
Will certificates be provided?
Yes, all participants receive verified certificates of participation. Outstanding delegates receive recognition awards. Certificates can be verified online through our Certificate Verification portal.
Is accommodation provided?
While accommodation is not included in the registration fee, we have partnered with nearby hotels to offer discounted rates for out-of-city delegates. Details will be shared after registration.
