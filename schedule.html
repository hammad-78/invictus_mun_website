Event Schedule

A detailed breakdown of all three days at Invictus MUN 2026.

Day 1 — July 24, 2026
08:00 – 09:00

Registration & Check-in

Delegate registration, credential collection, and welcome kits distribution.

Main Lobby
09:00 – 10:30

Opening Ceremony

Recitation, welcome address by the Secretary-General, keynote speech by the Chief Guest, and official conference inauguration.

Main Hall
10:30 – 11:00

Tea Break & Networking

Refreshments and an opportunity to meet fellow delegates and the Executive Board.

Lounge Area
11:00 – 13:00

Committee Session I

Roll call, setting the agenda, General Speakers' List, and opening statements by all delegates.

Committee Rooms
13:00 – 14:00

Lunch Break

Catered lunch for all delegates, EB members, and faculty advisors.

Dining Hall
14:00 – 16:30

Committee Session II

Moderated caucus, unmoderated caucus, and formulation of working papers.

Committee Rooms
16:30 – 17:00

Tea Break

Evening refreshments and informal discussions.

Lounge Area
17:00 – 19:00

Committee Session III

Working paper reviews, draft resolution writing, and intense debate.

Committee Rooms
19:30 – 21:00

Social Event / Delegates' Dinner

An evening of music, food, and networking to unwind after an intense first day.

Banquet Hall
Day 2 — July 25, 2026
09:00 – 09:30

Morning Assembly

Day 2 briefing, announcements, and committee room assignments.

Main Hall
09:30 – 12:00

Committee Session IV

Continued debate, crisis updates in applicable committees, and progress on working papers.

Committee Rooms
12:00 – 13:00

Lunch Break

Catered lunch for all delegates, EB members, and faculty advisors.

Dining Hall
13:00 – 15:30

Committee Session V

Alliance building, further crisis developments, and working paper submissions.

Committee Rooms
15:30 – 16:00

Tea Break

Evening refreshments and informal discussions.

Lounge Area
16:00 – 18:30

Committee Session VI

Draft resolution finalization, last calls for signatories, and final crisis updates.

Committee Rooms
19:30 – 21:00

Cultural Night

An evening of performances, music, and cultural celebration for all delegates.

Banquet Hall
Day 3 — July 26, 2026
09:00 – 09:30

Morning Assembly

Day 3 briefing and final announcements ahead of the concluding session.

Main Hall
09:30 – 12:00

Committee Session VII (Final Session)

Resolution presentation, voting procedure, final crisis conclusions, and committee closures.

Committee Rooms
12:00 – 13:00

Lunch Break

Final lunch with fellow delegates and last-minute farewells.

Dining Hall
13:00 – 14:30

Refreshments & Photo Sessions

Committee-wise group photographs and informal networking. Best Delegate selection is finalized.

Photo Area
15:00 – 17:00

Closing Ceremony

Committee reports, award distribution (Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate, Honorable Mention, Best Position Paper), closing address, and official conference conclusion.

Main Hall

Schedule Subject to Change

The above schedule is tentative and may be adjusted. Registered delegates will receive the final schedule via email prior to the event.
